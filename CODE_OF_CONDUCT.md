# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want WeddingGPT to be a welcoming place to work for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, in code review as much as anywhere else.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the couples who use it.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow them.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers of the
[selveapps](https://github.com/selveapps) organisation. All reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
